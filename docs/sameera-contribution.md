# Campus Food Service Assistant

## Contribution by Shaik Sameera

This contribution documents the campus food service workflow.

### Food Service Workflow

1. Student submits a food-related request or complaint.
2. The request is recorded in the system.
3. The responsible staff member reviews the request.
4. The issue is assigned for resolution.
5. The status is updated after resolution.
6. The student can track the request.
